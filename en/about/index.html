01Our perspective

The same commitment.
Across every decision.

Arconom is a construction company seeking to undertake responsibilities in construction, contracting, project management and construction management.

We consider design coordination, cost, programme, quality and delivery as interdependent parts of the same project. The working model is defined around the client’s needs and the conditions of each project.

02Our values

Technical discipline

An approach that considers the technical basis of decisions, information requirements and consequences for delivery.

Responsibility

Clear definition of contractual scope, authority and handover expectations from the outset.

Collaboration

Regular information exchange between the client, designers, engineers, suppliers and site teams.

Long-term value

Consider operational needs, maintenance requirements and building life alongside initial investment decisions.

03Our ambition

Quality projects.
Lasting partnerships.

We aim to build collaborations in Türkiye and international markets based on clear responsibilities and technical alignment. This is a growth ambition. Local conditions, regulations, resources and partnership structures must be assessed for each location and project.

04Resource-conscious decisions

Think in terms
of building life.

We aim to consider durable, fit-for-purpose materials, resource and waste planning during construction, maintenance access and whole-life costs. Environmental objectives are defined for each project and planned around measurable information.
