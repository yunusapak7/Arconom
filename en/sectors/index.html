ARCONOM / Sectors

Every building is different.
Our perspective is integrated.

Potential service areas span residential, commercial, workplace, industrial and logistics, and hospitality projects. Final scope depends on project requirements and company assessment.

These sectors are not a list of completed projects or references. Appointments are assessed against technical requirements, resources, local conditions and contractual scope.

Architectural selection · Not an Arconom project reference.

01

Residential

Residential projects considered through quality of life, construction discipline and investment objectives. Scope is shaped around typology, scale, shared spaces and handover requirements.

02

Commercial

Commercial spaces that bring user experience and operational requirements together. Tenant needs, technical infrastructure and phasing are reflected in the project programme.

03

Workplace

Office and workplace projects that respond to evolving ways of working. Spatial planning, building services, fit-out and commissioning interfaces are coordinated.

04

Industrial & logistics

Buildings shaped by production, storage and operational requirements. Process equipment interfaces, site access and business continuity are assessed for each project.

05

Hospitality

Projects that consider guest experience alongside operational needs. Operator requirements, spatial standards and opening milestones form part of the coordination brief.
