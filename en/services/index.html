ARCONOM / Services

Scope that fits your project.
Clear responsibilities.

We begin by understanding the brief, then define the role and deliverables together. The following areas form a service framework to be assessed for each project.

Service availability and scope are confirmed by proposal and contract following an assessment of project requirements and company resources. A service appointment does not automatically constitute a turnkey construction commitment.
01

Construction & general contracting

Translate design decisions into a planned, traceable and coordinated site delivery process.

Our potential role

Deliver the construction scope defined in the contract, coordinating work packages, site organisation and subcontractor interfaces.

Typical deliverables, subject to scope
  • Defined work packages and a delivery plan
  • Site progress and inspection records
  • Handover documentation for the contracted works
Discuss This Service
02

Project & construction management

Align disciplines, decisions and resources around shared project objectives.

Our potential role

Monitor scope, programme, cost and change on behalf of the client or within an agreed management structure.

Typical deliverables, subject to scope
  • Project management and responsibility plan
  • Master programme and decision schedule
  • Progress, risk and change reports
Discuss This Service
03

Design & engineering coordination

Bring architectural intent and engineering requirements together in a buildable solution.

Our potential role

Coordinate information flow, discipline interfaces and site requirements across design teams. Design responsibility remains with the appointed designers.

Typical deliverables, subject to scope
  • Interdisciplinary coordination schedule
  • Technical query and decision registers
  • Pre-construction document review checklists
Discuss This Service
04

Tendering, procurement & contractor coordination

Obtain clearly scoped proposals and base procurement decisions on comparable information.

Our potential role

Coordinate tender packages, technical and commercial bid comparisons, and procurement schedules. Decision-making and purchasing authority are defined by contract.

Typical deliverables, subject to scope
  • Work package and scope matrix
  • Technical and commercial bid comparisons
  • Procurement and contractor interface schedule
Discuss This Service
05

Cost, programme & progress monitoring

Understand current cost and programme performance, and identify decisions before they become critical.

Our potential role

Track actual performance against the approved budget and programme; report variances, potential impacts and available responses.

Typical deliverables, subject to scope
  • Budget and cost tracking schedules
  • Updated programme and variance analysis
  • Periodic client progress reports
Discuss This Service
06

Refurbishment & conversion

Adapt existing buildings to new requirements while responding to the realities of the site.

Our potential role

Coordinate existing-condition reviews, phasing and delivery. Required surveys, approvals and permits are defined for each project.

Typical deliverables, subject to scope
  • Existing-condition review and intervention scope
  • Phasing and implementation plan
  • Inspection, snagging and handover records
