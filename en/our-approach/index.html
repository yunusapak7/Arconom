Every step on site is connected to design, procurement and programme. Our working model makes these relationships visible and tracks the implications of decisions for delivery.

Rhythm, order and continuity.
01—06The project lifecycle, step by step

At every stage,
consider the next.

01

Understand the brief

Record objectives, stakeholders and priorities. Consider site and operational conditions that may affect health and safety from the outset.

02

Define the scope

Define scope, interfaces and required investigations. Establish an initial risk register and distinguish assumptions from verified information.

03

Plan programme & cost

Develop a master programme, budget assumptions and decision schedule. Review risks and contingency requirements with the client.

04

Coordinate design & procurement

Track design interfaces, technical queries and long-lead items. Define material and workmanship acceptance criteria within the agreed scope.

05

Deliver & monitor

Coordinate scope-appropriate site inspections, health and safety interfaces, non-conformances and change control. Provide regular client progress reports.

06

Handover & close out

Track snagging, required testing and inspections, handover records and operating information within scope. Close outstanding matters through a documented process.

07Controls across the whole process

Health & safety

Identify site and work-package risks early, define responsible parties and coordinate safety interfaces throughout delivery. Statutory and contractual duties are clarified for each project.

Quality control

Define acceptance criteria and inspection points; track material, workmanship and non-conformance records. Required tests are planned around project specifications.

Risk & change control

Maintain a record of risks, assumptions and scope changes. Present potential programme and cost impacts to the client before decisions are made.

Regular client reporting

Share progress, cost, programme, open technical matters and decisions required within an agreed reporting structure. Frequency and content are established at the outset.

This working model is adapted to each project and contract. Authority, approval procedures and responsibilities are defined before the appointment begins.
